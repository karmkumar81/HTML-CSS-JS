About CHARUSAT University

CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science and Technology) is a private university located at Changa, Anand, Gujarat, India. The university offers diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs in Engineering, Pharmacy, Computer Applications, Management, Nursing, Physiotherapy and many other fields.

The university provides quality education, modern laboratories, experienced faculty members and various opportunities for research, innovation and student development.
